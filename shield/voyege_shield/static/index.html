About Voyege

Security & fraud,
caught the moment your platform changes.

Voyege is an agentic, real-time security and fraud shield for online booking and travel platforms. Every time your app changes, it re-checks the rules that move money and guard data — then proves each problem safely and hands your team a ready-to-fix ticket. It finds the business-logic flaws that ordinary scanners structurally cannot see.

What Voyege does

One shield, five jobs — the parts ordinary tools miss, built for how travel platforms actually lose money.

₹

Business-rule engine

Checks the rules that move money — refund ≤ paid, charged = quoted, coupon limits, credit caps — against what your platform actually stored.

⇄

Multi-agency access checker

Tests as two agencies at once to catch cross-tenant reads and privilege jumps a single-identity scan never sees.

◉

Zombie & shadow inventory

Maps every endpoint from traffic and code, labels forgotten and undocumented ones, and exports an OpenAPI spec.

🛡

Provable safety gate

One read-only path to the target: scope allowlist, rate limits, kill switch and a tamper-evident log of every request.

✓

Verify, rank & ticket

Each finding is re-proved independently, priced in ₹ at risk, and sent to the right owner — with auto-retest after the fix.

👁

Fraud signals in logs

Watches your own logs for abuse already in progress and flags "seen in traffic" on the ticket.

How it works

A single loop runs on every change; only one step ever touches the platform being tested.

Detect change

A deploy, a new JS bundle, a shifted response or a traffic change starts a run within seconds.

Discover & plan

Build the API map, then plan targeted checks for exactly what changed — not a blind spray.

Test safely

Run the checks through the safety gate on synthetic data, read-only by default.

Prove & ticket

Re-prove each finding, rank it in ₹, ticket the owner, and retest after the fix.
